Self Check-In by CarMax

UX/UI Designer, Researcher, Wireframing, Visual Designer, & Prototyping

Overview

In 2021, CarMax introduced the Instant Offer Tool, which allowed customers to receive an offer on their vehicle online. This self-check-in tool allowed customers to self-service when coming to the store to get their offer verified.

In higher volume stores, customers experience long wait times (up to 3 -5 hours) to be checked in by an associate, not including the actual car-selling process.

The Problem

CarMax's Instant Offer Tool has been very successful, leading to significant wait times for customers. Additionally, there is no transparency regarding customers' status in the appraisal process.

Three hour average wait times: In higher volume stores, customers experience long wait times for check-in by an associate, not including the actual car-selling process.

How Did We Identify the Problem?

To identify the problem, we observed associates and customers during store visits and collected feedback from them through interviews.

Differentiating the Problem

We collected and reviewed all of the data and used the solution tree method to plan how to solve the problem and conduct a quick MVP test.

Self Check-In MVP Test Plan

We provided Instant Offer customers with two self check-in options: an email link sent the morning of their appointment and a QR code displayed on signage inside the store.

Test Objectives:
Provide customers with self service check-in options.

Learning goals:

What percentage of customers will use self check-in?
Does removing the appraisal check-in volume increase Associate efficiency?

Test goals: 50% of IO customers will opt to use a self-service option.

The two methods customers could use to check-in via QR code or through the store lobby kiosk terminal to complete the report of the visit.

The customer completed the form in Ethnio to check-in.

Once customers completed the form, we manually entered them into the microsite which begins the vehicle verification process, along with help the associate locate the customer’s vehicle.

Opportunities

We’ll need to provide a more productized way where customers aren’t able to check-in prior to their arrival.

Placement of the signs will be important therefore it doesn’t impact the greeting of other customers not checking in.

Check-in language will need to be tweaked, work with UX writer to help use intentional language that’ll help direct customers.

We’ll need to make sure that customers are able to reopen the check-in form if closed or not fully completed.

Training and clear communication with associates (Sales Consultants, Business Office, etc.) to streamline implementation of initiative will be needed.

We’ll need to provide additional options for customers to check-in other than their personal mobile device (I.e., tablet, iPad, etc.)

Gearing Up for the XF Store Test

Opportunity:
As an appraisal customer, I feel disappointed with the long wait times to get car appraised. As CarMax, we'd like to have as many customers use Self Check-In as possible.

Hypothesis:
We believe by providing customers with a self-service way to begin their vehicle appraisal, we can decrease the pressure on the Associates while increasing customer satisfaction.

We began planning for how we would conduct the test being that an XF store is setup differently than a traditional store.

We began solutioning on how to prevent early check-in and other things that would cause a less than ideal experience.

We continued to conduct interviews with XF stores to understand customer pain points around wait times and the selling experience.

XF Store Test Plan

We placed signs with QR codes in the parking lot. Also, signs were places around the entrances in case a line develops at "the point".

We used Microsoft forms to mimic check-in flow.

Limitations: Couldn't track customers through to Flow from Microsoft Forms.

When a customer with an appraisal appointment checks in, immediately move them to the Checked-In section at The Point. No need to auto assign, the Coordinator will do this.

Coordinator would like to see the TYPE called out if the customer self checks in so they understand how they showed up in Salesforce.

If a customer checks in without an appointment, add them to Salesforce with the “Add Customer“ flow. Only Name and Phone required, notes are optional.

During the Test

The goal of self check-in is to provide customers with an option to get progress if they'd rather. There was a line at the tent, and it wasn't customers for customers to complete the check-in.

General Learnings

Store Hours:
Due to paperwork, teams are having to turn away walk-ins after a certain time before store closing due to not being able to complete the sale in time. Team will be incorporating this into the check-in app to direct customers to come inside for check-in if the store is not yet open or too soon to closing.

Differences between IO & Final Offer:
Associates see the IO value when checking into the Microsite. If they no longer do this step, they can’t see the IO value anymore to know if this is the same/different from final appraisal.

Collecting payoffs:
Associates use the same QR code when Checking-In a customer if they weren't self-servicing on their own, and it seemed to build trust with the customer just as well as a normal check-in process.

Check-in needs to be for everyone:
A lane customers were using check-in too.

True Appraisal Time:
Stores are adding customers to the Salesforce waitlist before the IO Microsite queue. The appraisal time data we pull starts at the time of check-in at the IO Microsite so we aren’t seeing the true length of time the customer has been waiting to begin their verification. Self check-in will add customers to the IO Microsite immediately and therefore we’ll see the true length of wait time.

Estimated Wait Times:
Very important for customers but this is generated by the associates with multiple variables. Is there a possible tech solution that make sense?

Parking is chaos:
Due to limited parking availability, people park anywhere. Parking also varies from store to store.

Email/pre-communication:
Email is not working but maybe we test text if we do pre-arrival communication.

Self Check-In Launch

On September 27th, we launched the Self Check-In Product at a traditional store in Ft. Myers, Florida.

More than 50% of customers used the new app all three days we were there, and it improved their wait times and more than 75% of customers checked in without walking into the store.

As for me, I really enjoyed working on this project. I couldn’t have had a better team to work with. The teamwork was amazing and in addition to that, having a project that not only required research from the customer, it required us to work with associates, as well. I learned a lot, and felt myself grow as a product designer. Unfortunately, right after launch, the team found out it was splitting up, and the product was going to another team. It would’ve been nice to continue to iterate and improve the process.
